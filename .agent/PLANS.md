# Execution Plans

Create a plan for work that spans multiple components, contributors, or verification stages. Small single-file edits do not need one.

Plan filenames use `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`.

## Template

```markdown
# Objective

## Scope

## Out of scope

## Steps

- [ ] First verifiable step

## Acceptance criteria

## Verification

## Risks and rollback
```

Keep current plans in `plans/active/`. Move completed or cancelled plans to `plans/archive/` and record the outcome.
